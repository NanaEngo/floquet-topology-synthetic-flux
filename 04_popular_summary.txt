Title: Finite-size and loss-profile limits on topological protection of Wigner negativity in a chiral Floquet SSH chain

Topological phases are often expected to make quantum states more robust against noise. We test that expectation for a non-Gaussian resource—the negativity of a quantum Wigner function—in a periodically driven bosonic chain. We first verify the topological classification of the coherent model, then compare matched dissipative simulations at several system sizes and loss profiles. A positive small-system contrast disappears and changes sign as the chain grows, while uniform loss removes the contrast. The sign pattern for the five-cell size was set as a prediction before measurement and matched on all four conditions, and the small-chain negativity is verified without any Wigner grid by displaced-state witnesses. A second test follows the Floquet edge mode through its micromotion and also fails to establish a passive survival advantage. The results do not show topological protection of Wigner negativity. Instead, they identify a concrete boundary between a topological invariant of a coherent model and the behavior of a nonlinear open-system observable. This distinction provides a reproducible benchmark for future studies of topological resources in continuous-variable quantum platforms.
